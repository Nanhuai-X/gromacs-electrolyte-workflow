# RESP and RESP1 boundary

RESP charge fitting requires a defined quantum method, basis, geometry set, ESP grid, molecular charge and multiplicity, equivalence constraints, restraint protocol, and charge closure. The registry stores input and output hashes, atom mapping, and fit diagnostics.

RESP1 and RESP2 are distinct protocols. The Skill may prepare a plan or validate an existing run, but it does not invent Gaussian data or silently refit a molecule.
