# MSD and diffusion

Unwrap trajectories with a tested PBC procedure and use consistent center-of-mass handling. Diagnose ballistic, caged or subdiffusive, and diffusive regimes before fitting. For 3D Einstein diffusion, the slope of MSD versus time is 6D after unit conversion. Report fit window, slope, R2, block consistency, uncertainty, and whether the data support a production-quality coefficient. A short visually linear segment is not enough.
