# Scientific limitations

This Skill standardizes reproducible workflow decisions; it does not establish that a force field is scientifically correct. Bulk density, RDF, coordination, transport, and cluster metrics are validation evidence with finite-size, time-scale, sampling, and model-form limits. Solvent charge closure, ionic charge scaling, and RESP or RESP2 sensitivity require explicit source and uncertainty discussion. Never promote a preliminary pass to a universal force-field claim.
