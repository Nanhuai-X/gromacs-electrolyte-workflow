# CP2K 2026.2 rules

## Manual gate

Use the exact branch recorded in `assets/template_registry.json`:

<https://manual.cp2k.org/cp2k-2026_2-branch/CP2K_INPUT.html>

Record the branch, retrieval time, content hash, consulted sections, and the
matching CP2K executable in `manual_manifest.yaml`.

## Version-specific checks

- Keep the 2026.2 nested DOS/CURVE/PDOS layout in the 2026.2 family only.
- Validate virtual-state controls and the standard-diagonalization property
  recipe against the exact manual and binary.
- Keep CPU/GPU build evidence separate. GPU availability, a GPU-enabled build,
  and actual GPU utilization are different facts.
- Do not transfer a wavefunction restart across CP2K versions or incompatible
  build families without an explicit compatibility test.

The included templates are skeletons. A manual entry or successful process
return does not establish a supported property; expected artifacts and
property-specific checks are still required.
